12 Signs It's Time for 24-Hour Care

The safety, health and caregiver warning signs that indicate a loved one needs round-the-clock support — and what to do next.

By the 247hrsCare Editorial Team · Updated 2026-09-28 · 6 min read ·

The move to 24-hour care rarely happens on a calendar. It usually follows a pattern of near-misses. If several of these signs sound familiar, it is time to plan — before a crisis makes the decision for you.

Safety signs

  1. Two or more falls in the last six months, or fear of falling that stops them moving.
  2. Getting up at night and becoming confused or unsteady.
  3. Wandering, or trying to leave home at unusual hours.
  4. Leaving the stove, taps or doors on or unlocked.
  5. Missing or doubling medications.

Health signs

  1. Weight loss, dehydration or spoiled food in the fridge.
  2. Incontinence that is not being managed.
  3. Repeated hospital or emergency visits.
  4. A new diagnosis that will progress (e.g. advanced dementia, Parkinson's, ALS, heart failure).

Caregiver signs

  1. The family caregiver is not sleeping, is ill, or is missing work.
  2. Family members disagree or feel resentful.
  3. You are worried every time the phone rings.
If there is immediate danger — a fall with injury, chest pain, sudden confusion or signs of stroke — call 911 or your local emergency number first.

What to do next

  1. Write down the specific incidents and times of day they happen.
  2. Ask the doctor for a functional assessment and any home-health referral.
  3. Take our care needs quiz to see which care level fits.
  4. Compare 24-hour shift care, live-in care and residential options with the cost calculator.
  5. Request a free care match to speak with vetted providers.
