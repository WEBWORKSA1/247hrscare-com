Live-In Care vs 24-Hour Care: Which Is Right?

A side-by-side comparison of live-in care and 24-hour shift care — supervision, sleep rules, continuity and cost.

By the 247hrsCare Editorial Team · Updated 2026-09-28 · 5 min read ·

Both options put a caregiver in the home around the clock, but they are built for different needs. Choosing wrong either overpays for coverage you do not need, or leaves night-time gaps that are unsafe.

Live-in care24-hour shift care
Caregivers1 primary (plus relief)2–3 rotating per day
Night coveragePresent but sleepingAwake and alert
Best forSleeps through most nightsFrequent night needs, high fall risk, late-stage dementia
ContinuityHigh — one familiar faceModerate — several caregivers
CostLower daily rateHighest — every hour billed
Home needsPrivate bedroom for caregiverNone beyond care space

The one-question test

How many times per night does your loved one need hands-on help? Zero or one: live-in care is usually fine. Two or more, or unpredictable: choose 24-hour shift care. Awake care is also the right choice when someone must be watched continuously (for example, high wandering risk).

Hybrid options

Many families use daytime care plus a few awake overnights per week, or live-in care with an extra awake night caregiver during a difficult period. Care plans should be reviewed every 60–90 days as needs change.
