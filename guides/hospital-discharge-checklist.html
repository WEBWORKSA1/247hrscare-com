Hospital to Home: The Discharge Checklist That Prevents Readmission

What to ask before discharge, how to prepare the home, and how to organise the first 30 days after a hospital stay.

By the 247hrsCare Editorial Team · Updated 2026-09-28 · 5 min read ·

The weeks after a hospital stay are a vulnerable time. A clear plan before discharge is the single best way to avoid a return trip.

Before leaving the hospital

  • Get the written discharge plan and a reconciled medication list (new, changed and stopped medications).
  • Ask which warning signs mean "call the doctor" vs "call 911".
  • Confirm follow-up appointments and who is booking them.
  • Ask whether home health, therapy or equipment has been ordered — and when it will arrive.
  • Clarify activity limits: lifting, stairs, bathing, driving.

Preparing the home

  • Set up a recovery space on one floor if possible.
  • Install night-lights and clear walkways.
  • Arrange a shower chair, raised toilet seat or commode if advised.
  • Stock easy, nutritious meals and fluids.

The first 30 days

  • Arrange someone to stay for the first 48–72 hours at minimum.
  • Keep a daily log: medications, meals, fluids, sleep, pain and symptoms.
  • Attend the first follow-up visit within 7–14 days, or as instructed.
  • Consider short-term post-hospital care if family cannot cover all hours.
