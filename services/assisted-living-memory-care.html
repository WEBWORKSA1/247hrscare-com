Assisted Living & Memory Care: Compare Communities

When care needs outgrow what is realistic at home — or isolation becomes the bigger risk — a residential community can offer 24/7 staff, meals, activities and social life. Memory care communities add secure environments and dementia-specialised staff.

Get matched for assisted living & memory careEstimate costs

Who is assisted living & memory care for?

  • People needing daily help but not continuous medical care
  • Those isolated at home or struggling with meals and home upkeep
  • Dementia with wandering risk (memory care)
  • Couples with different care needs who want to live nearby

What's typically included

Private or shared apartments
24-hour staff and emergency response
Meals, housekeeping and laundry
Help with ADLs and medication management
Social, fitness and cognitive programmes
Secure units and specialised staff in memory care

What does it cost?

The U.S. national median for assisted living was about $6,200 per month in 2025 (CareScout). Memory care typically adds a premium. Nursing homes, which provide 24-hour skilled nursing, run about $315/day semi-private and $355/day private.

Costs and coverage rules vary by location and change over time. Confirm with providers and programmes directly.

Frequently asked questions

What should we ask on a tour?

Staff-to-resident ratios by shift, staff turnover, how care levels are assessed and priced, what happens if needs increase, recent inspection reports, and all fees beyond base rent.

Does a referral service cost families money?

Reputable referral services are free to families because communities pay them a fee. Always ask how they are compensated — see our How We Make Money page.
